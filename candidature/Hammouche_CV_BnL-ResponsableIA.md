## Responsable projets IA et Data

Since 2014, I have designed and deployed production AI systems across government, healthcare, and financial services: NLP pipelines processing millions of digitised documents, multimodal RAG platforms, predictive models validated by clinical teams. Each one involved sustained work with domain specialists — from requirements to handover — because a system that runs but isn't used has delivered nothing.

The valorisation of heritage collections through AI is a problem I have been working on directly. The Intelligent Luxembourg Heritage project, built on BnL's CC0 open data, produced a measured result: CER 2.72% against the published Nautilus/Kraken baseline, zero-shot, on the official benchmark images. The three-gate roadmap is structured under QUAPITAL-HERMES, composed with CRISP-ML(Q) for the AI lifecycle. I want to bring that work into BnL and develop it in close collaboration with the teams who know the collections and the researchers who depend on them.
